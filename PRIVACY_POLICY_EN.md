# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 12, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

AncientKey ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Location Data
- With your permission, we collect location data to provide map and navigation services.
- Location data is used solely for displaying maps and calculating distances to historical sites.
- We do not store or transmit your location data to external servers.

### Device Information
- We may collect device information such as device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and crash reporting.

### Local Data
- Favorites, notes, and user preferences are stored locally on your device.
- This data is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

### Photos and Media
- If you use photo features, images are processed locally on your device.
- We do not upload or store your photos on any server.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide map and location-based services
- Display nearby ancient historical sites
- Save your favorites and preferences locally
- Improve user experience and app functionality
- Provide customer support

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All user data is stored locally on your device
- Data is protected using device-level encryption
- We do not maintain external databases containing your personal information
- We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data

## 5. Third-Party Services

Our Application may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

- **Map Services**: We use mapping services to display historical maps. These services may collect anonymized usage data according to their privacy policies.
- **Analytics**: We may use analytics services to understand app usage patterns. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data stored in the app
- Delete your data by clearing app data or uninstalling
- Opt-out of location services through device settings
- Request information about data processing

## 8. Data Retention

Since data is stored locally on your device:
- Data persists until you clear app data or uninstall the application
- We do not retain any personal data on external servers

## 9. International Users

If you are accessing the Application from outside Turkey, please be aware that your information may be processed in accordance with Turkish data protection laws.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
