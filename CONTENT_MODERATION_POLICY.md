# Content Moderation Policy

**Effective Date: January 19, 2026**

## Overview

AncientKey maintains a **ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY** for objectionable content and abusive user behavior. This policy outlines our commitment to maintaining a safe, respectful, and harassment-free community for all users.

## Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on AncientKey:

### 1. Abusive & Harassing Content
- Threats, intimidation, or bullying directed at other users
- Harassment based on protected characteristics (race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability)
- Personal attacks or insults
- Doxxing (sharing personal information without consent)

### 2. Hate Speech & Discrimination
- Hateful language targeting individuals or groups
- Content promoting discrimination or violence
- Slurs or dehumanizing language

### 3. Sexual & Adult Content
- Sexually explicit material
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) - will be reported to law enforcement
- Inappropriate sexual comments or solicitation

### 4. Spam & Misleading Content
- Spam, scams, or phishing attempts
- Deliberately misleading or false information
- Commercial spam or unwanted advertising
- Fake news or misinformation

### 5. Intellectual Property Violations
- Copyright infringement
- Trademark violations
- Plagiarism

### 6. Privacy Violations
- Sharing others' personal information without consent
- Doxxing
- Sharing someone's private messages or images without consent

## Reporting Process

### How to Report Content

Users can report objectionable content by:
1. Clicking the **"Report" button** on any comment, note, or photo
2. Providing a detailed reason for the report
3. Submitting the report

### What Happens After Reporting

1. **Initial Review (Within 24 hours)**: Our moderation team reviews the report
2. **Investigation**: We determine if the content violates this policy
3. **Action**: If violation confirmed:
   - Content is removed immediately
   - User receives a warning or suspension notice
   - Multiple violations result in account termination

## User Blocking

Users can **block other users** to:
- Prevent that user from interacting with their content
- Hide that user's content from their feed
- Automatically notify administrators of problematic behavior

When a user is blocked, the blocking is logged and reviewed by administrators to ensure enforcement of this policy.

## Consequences for Violations

### First Violation
- Content removal
- Formal warning
- Account restrictions may apply

### Second Violation
- Content removal
- Account temporary suspension (7-30 days)
- Loss of certain privileges

### Repeated Violations
- Permanent account suspension
- Possible reporting to law enforcement (for serious crimes)
- User is ejected from the platform

## Our Commitment

- **24-Hour Response Time**: All reports are reviewed and addressed within 24 hours
- **Transparency**: We document all moderation actions
- **Fairness**: All decisions are made objectively and consistently
- **Appeals**: Users can appeal moderation decisions

## Special Cases

### Child Safety
AncientKey has **ZERO TOLERANCE** for content involving minors. Any such content will be:
- Immediately removed
- User permanently banned
- Reported to appropriate law enforcement agencies (NCMEC, local authorities)

### Illegal Content
Content violating laws will be:
- Immediately removed
- User reported to law enforcement
- Reported to relevant agencies (FBI, Interpol, etc.)

## Developer Responsibility

AncientKey developers are committed to:
- Reviewing all reports within the stated timeframe
- Taking appropriate action against violating content
- Maintaining detailed records of moderation actions
- Cooperating with law enforcement when necessary
- Continuously improving moderation tools and processes
